# Trademark preparation notes

These are preparation notes for a possible future trademark application. **Nothing has been filed.** This is not legal advice. Before filing, confirm with an industrial property attorney or the official office.

## Signs that could be protected

| Sign | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **OptimusZuka** | word mark | creator name, used on YouTube and GitHub |
| **ZukaForce** | word mark + figurative (logo) | logo with steering wheel, black/yellow/white (`assets/logo.png`) |
| **ZF** icon | figurative | gear + steering wheel + "ZF" (`assets/realffb.png`, `assets/realffb.ico`) |
| **FS25 RealFFB** | — | **not recommended as a registered mark**: "FS25" refers to Farming Simulator 25, a trademark of GIANTS Software. Keep using it only as a descriptive product name ("RealFFB for FS25") and register your own signs instead. |

## Where

- **Portugal**: INPI Portugal (national mark).
- **European Union**: EUIPO (one EU trade mark for all member states).
- **Brazil**: INPI Brasil.
- **Several countries at once**: the Madrid System (WIPO), based on a national or EU application.

## Classes (Nice Classification) to consider

- **9**: downloadable software, computer programs.
- **41**: entertainment, online videos (YouTube channel).
- **42**: software design and development services.

## Before filing

1. Search for existing similar marks in TMview (EU) and the WIPO Global Brand Database, plus the INPI Brasil database.
2. Keep proof of creation of the logos: the original files with their dates, and who made them. If someone else or a tool made them, you need a written transfer or confirmation of rights.
3. Keep proof of use: release pages, YouTube videos and dated screenshots showing the signs.
4. Check fees and deadlines on each office's official site.
